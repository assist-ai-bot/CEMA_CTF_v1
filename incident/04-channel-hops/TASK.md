# Task 4 — Channel hops

`hop_record.txt` is a tab-separated scanner log: time, the frequency the scanner showed (`center_khz`), and signal strength (`snr_db`). The control link was hopping. The recording stops before hop 36.

The flag is the corrected center frequency of hop 36, in kHz.

Submit it in this shape and no other. The number below is an example of the characters, not the frequency:

```
Intern_Pro_Max{100000}
```

Digits only. No units, decimal point, comma, or spaces.
